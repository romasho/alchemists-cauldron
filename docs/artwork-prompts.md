# Artwork prompts

The original eleven prompts are below. The two additional Hard assets use the [Hard ingredient prompts](hard-artwork-prompts.md).

All 13 images were generated with the built-in image_gen tool (no CLI). Transparent sprites preserve generated alpha. Project copies are WebP at quality 88, cropped to their alpha bounds and downscaled with Lanczos; no generated text is used in the interface. The original combined scene from the first iteration has been replaced by these independent layers.

## workshop-background

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background layer for fantasy alchemy game. Create a wide 3:2 landscape illustration of a dark medieval alchemist workshop matching the supplied dark fantasy reference: aged timber shelves, tarnished bronze, stone workbench, glass bottles on distant walls, a blue moonlit arched window at upper right, subtle candles near the edges. Center 55% is dark open negative space with a low stone hearth, ready to place a separate cauldron image. Rich painterly realistic game art, atmospheric shadows. NO cauldron, NO glowing liquid, NO foreground book, NO UI panels, NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO buttons, NO frames, NO watermark. Background scene only.

Match dark fantasy painterly realistic game art: tarnished bronze ornamental scrollwork around dark iron belly, small alchemical triangle medallion center, side ring handles, luminous green reflected light on upper rim and inside. IMPORTANT: cauldron is EMPTY, dark hollow interior, NO potion, NO liquid, NO steam, NO bubbles, NO particles, NO scene, NO pedestal, NO ground, NO text, NO UI. Broad bowl silhouette, almost round belly. Object fills canvas with modest transparent margin. Mouth opening at 25% of image height, width 72% of image width, open ellipse height 12% of image height. Real transparent background.
